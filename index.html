The Story So Far

Product overview · premise

Somewhere past the Great Lava Wall, a chicken named Colonel Cluck had a vision: a valley where crops harvest themselves, sugar ships itself, and nobody — nobody — has to climb thirty flights of stairs ever again.

Twelve engineers answered the call. They wired observers to pistons, taught daylight sensors to tell the time, and built a teleport network so fast you can visit every farm before your bread finishes baking. They built the 30-storey Chicken Co Tower, an Iron Golem the size of a building whose eyes light up at night, and a solid-gold Plinko Palace for when the day's work is done.

Now the valley needs a new Farm Manager. Your job: keep the automated lines running, sell fresh produce at the Farm-to-Table Market, and earn enough Cluckcoins to become Employee of the Month before the shift timer runs out. Easy, right?

…Right?

“Farm workers need conditions like real modern work standards — beds, light, space — or the system just doesn't work.”— a Level Designer, Week 2 project log (and the reason every villager in the valley has a window)

🎯 Who it's for

Players aged 10–15 who are curious how real automated farms and factories work, and teachers who want a playable demo of sensors, logic and feedback loops.

🧩 The problem it tackles

Automation is everywhere (supermarkets, farms, factories), but the logic behind it is invisible. Cluckwork Valley shows how it works: every system has a glass wall, a sign that explains it, and a lamp that lights up when it runs.

Player Guide

Rules · controls · instructions
🏁

Goal

Earn 250 Cluckcoins before the 10-minute shift timer hits zero and you become Employee of the Month, with fireworks.

💀

Lose condition

Timer hits 0 with less than 250 coins: “The chickens have unionised. Try again.” You're sent back to the Hub, no hard feelings.

🔁

Reset

Pull the gold lever at the Hub. Coins, timer and farm stock all reset and the arena floor rebuilds itself.

📜 How to play — your first shift

  1. Spawn at the Hub. Read the welcome board (big text, high contrast). The sidebar shows your Cluckcoins and Shift timer.
  2. Press the green START button. A title says “Shift started!”, a bell rings, and the Hub lamp turns green.
  3. Pick a teleporter pad. Each pad is colour-and-shape coded to match the map. Step on it and you're there.
  4. Collect produce from the output chests at each farm. Read the glass-wall signs to see how each machine works.
  5. Sell at the Farm-to-Table Market. 16 wheat = 5 coins · 16 sugar = 6 coins · 16 dried kelp = 8 coins · 16 potatoes = 5 coins.
  6. Spend or save. Buy food at the shops (you'll get hungry), or play Plinko with free Golden Feathers.
  7. Hit 250 coins and get to the top of Chicken Co Tower for your promotion. 🎉

Under the Hood

Technical showcase · algorithms

Every automated system in the valley follows the same pattern: a sensor notices something → logic decides → something happens → the player is told. Here is how each one works, with the real commands.

🔄 The Harvest Loop: how the whole valley connects

Self-harvesting sugar cane sensor + iteration

Sugar cane grows on its own. An observer faces the 2nd block of each stalk. When the cane grows into that space, the observer fires a one-tick pulse and the piston breaks the stalk. The bottom block stays, so it keeps growing again: a natural loop.

Dropped cane floats down a water stream into a hopper. A comparator reads the hopper and pulses a command block that adds 1 to the sugar scoreboard. At 64 the mill “ships” a crate to the Sugar Shop.

Why observers, not a clock? A clock fires even when nothing has grown. An observer only fires when it needs to, so there's less lag and no half-grown cane gets cut.

# One-time setup (impulse block)
scoreboard objectives add sugar dummy "Sugar Cane"
scoreboard objectives add stock dummy "Shop Stock"

# Comparator pulse → count each item (chain)
scoreboard players add Farm sugar 1

# SELECTION — repeating block checks the threshold
execute if score Farm sugar matches 64.. run scoreboard players add Farm stock 1
execute if score Farm sugar matches 64.. run playsound random.levelup @a[tag=shift]
execute if score Farm sugar matches 64.. run title @a[tag=shift] actionbar §e🍬 Sugar shipment sent to the shop!
# reset LAST, otherwise the lines above never see 64
execute if score Farm sugar matches 64.. run scoreboard players set Farm sugar 0

# ELSE — live progress on the actionbar for nearby players
execute unless score Farm sugar matches 64.. run titleraw @a[r=20] actionbar {"rawtext":[{"text":"§aSugar: "},{"score":{"name":"Farm","objective":"sugar"}},{"text":" / 64"}]}

The Night-Eyed Golem NOT gate · pure redstone

A daylight sensor normally gives a strong signal in the day. We flipped it into inverted mode (right-click it and it turns blue), which works like a NOT gate: IF it is NOT day THEN power the lamps.

The signal runs through hidden redstone in the golem's neck to two redstone lamps behind red glass: the eyes. At sunset they light up and the Iron Works sign says “Night shift: open.”

The hard bit: fitting the wiring inside a statue without it showing. The final version runs dust up the inside of the back wall and uses a repeater to keep the signal strong for 18 blocks.

// Logic (truth table)
daylight?   inverted output   eyes
 YES (day)      0             OFF
 NO (night)    15             ON 👀

// Pseudocode
LOOP every game tick
    IF NOT daytime THEN
        lamps ← ON
        sign  ← "Night shift: open"
    ELSE
        lamps ← OFF
    END IF
END LOOP

9-stop teleport network command chains

Each pad at the Hub has a pressure plate on top of an impulse command block with three chain blocks behind it. Step on it and four things happen in order: you teleport (facing the zone entrance so you're never lost), hear the portal sound, see the zone name, and the destination lamp blinks.

Every zone has a return pad back to the Hub, so you can't get stranded.

Chaos Chicken Hour: every 5 minutes one random player who has opted in gets warped to the valley centre for a surprise mini-event. A repeating block with a 6000-tick delay runs the timer.

# Impulse (pressure plate) → Sugar Mill pad
tp @p -182 66 41 facing -182 66 30
# Chain (always active)
playsound mob.endermen.portal @p
title @p actionbar §a🎋 Sugar Mill — follow the GREEN hexagons
setblock -178 68 41 redstone_block   # blink lamp

# Chaos Chicken Hour — repeating, delay 6000 ticks (5 min)
tp @r[tag=chaos] 0 80 0
# v0.2 bug: was tp @a every tick 😱 → see Bug Board #3

Chicken Co Tower elevator variables + 29-block chain

No-one should climb 30 floors. Step onto the lift pad and press ▲ or ▼ to change your personal floor score. It's clamped to 1–30 with range checks (31.. and ..0), and the actionbar shows your choice. Press GO.

GO fires a chain of 29 command blocks, one for each floor you can travel to. Each one tests if score @s floor matches N, so only the right one teleports you. A level-up “ding” plays and the floor name pops up: “Floor 17: Egg Accounting.”

Improvement idea (v2): work out the Y-coordinate from the floor number so one block replaces 29. We kept the chain because it lets every floor have its own custom welcome message.

# ▲ button
scoreboard players add @p floor 1
execute as @p if score @s floor matches 31.. run scoreboard players set @s floor 30

# ▼ button
scoreboard players remove @p floor 1
execute as @p if score @s floor matches ..0 run scoreboard players set @s floor 1

# GO → chain of 29 (floors 2–30). Floor N is at y = 64 + 5N
execute as @p[r=3] if score @s floor matches 2  run tp @s 210 74 55
execute as @p[r=3] if score @s floor matches 3  run tp @s 210 79 55
  …
execute as @p[r=3] if score @s floor matches 17 run tp @s 210 149 55
  …
execute as @p[r=3] if score @s floor matches 30 run tp @s 210 214 55
playsound random.levelup @p

Farm-to-Table Market selection + variables

The market is the centre of the game. The SELL button checks whether you're holding enough produce with hasitem. Only if you are does it take the items and pay you. The BUY button checks your coins first.

QA lesson: in v0.2 the “not enough coins” message ran after we'd already taken the coins, so people who could just afford something saw an error and got charged. Moving the unless check to run first fixed it. Order matters!

The market only sells from the farms' own stock. If the sugar mill stops, the shop runs out, so players can see the supply chain at work.

# SELL 16 wheat → +5 coins (impulse → conditional chain)
execute as @p if entity @s[hasitem={item=wheat,quantity=16..}] run scoreboard players add @s coins 5
clear @p wheat 0 16          # conditional: only runs if the line above worked
playsound random.orb @p

# BUY 3 bread for 10 coins (the Sugar Shop works the same way at 6)
# ELSE branch runs FIRST, then a tag remembers who passed the check
execute as @p unless score @s coins matches 10.. run title @s actionbar §c✖ Need 10 Cluckcoins
execute as @p if score @s coins matches 10.. run tag @s add buying
give @a[tag=buying] bread 3
scoreboard players remove @a[tag=buying] coins 10
tag @a remove buying

# Shift timer — repeating, delay 20 ticks (1 s)
scoreboard players remove Clock shift 1
execute if score Clock shift matches 0 as @a[tag=shift] if score @s coins matches 250.. run title @s title §6🏆 Employee of the Month!
execute if score Clock shift matches 0 as @a[tag=shift] unless score @s coins matches 250.. run title @s title §cThe chickens have unionised.

Knockback Arena game loop · win/lose/reset

Walk through the arena gate (a tripwire) and you're tagged arena and handed a knockback stick. Outside the arena, a repeating block gives everyone without that tag Weakness 255, so punches do zero damage there. The safe zone really is safe.

Fall into a pit (below y = 40) and you're out: teleported to the spectator stand with a cheerful “KNOCKED OUT 🐣”. Each tick the arena counts the players left. When the count is 1, that player wins.

The reset lever uses /fill to rebuild the floor and clears dropped items, so the next round can start straight away.

# Safe zones everywhere else (repeating)
effect @a[tag=!arena] weakness 2 255 true

# DEFEAT — fell in a pit, anywhere below y = 40 (repeating + chain)
# tag first, teleport last, so every step still finds the player
tag @a[tag=arena,y=-64,dy=104] add out
title @a[tag=out] title §cKNOCKED OUT 🐣
tag @a[tag=out] remove arena
tp @a[tag=out] 340 72 160
tag @a[tag=out] remove out

# ITERATION — count who's left, every tick
scoreboard players set Arena alive 0
execute as @a[tag=arena] run scoreboard players add Arena alive 1

# VICTORY
execute if score Arena alive matches 1 run title @a[tag=arena] title §6🏆 LAST CHICKEN STANDING

# RESET lever
fill 320 45 140 360 45 180 white_concrete
kill @e[type=item,x=320,y=40,z=140,dx=40,dy=20,dz=40]

Agent “Clucky” replants the Rest Field MakeCode Python · nested loops

Type replant in chat and our Agent robot plants the 9 × 9 field row by row, using a nested loop. Before it tills, it checks the block below. If something is already growing there, it skips that spot so it never digs up crops.

The field is split into three strips that take turns to rest (see Fair Play below). The Agent only replants the strip whose turn it is, which is stored in a variable.

rest_strip = 0   # 0, 1 or 2 — which strip is resting

def plant_row(length):
    for i in range(length):
        if not agent.detect(AgentDetection.BLOCK, DOWN):
            agent.till(DOWN)
            agent.place(DOWN)
        agent.move(FORWARD, 1)

def on_chat():
    global rest_strip
    agent.teleport_to_player()
    agent.set_slot(1)              # slot 1 = wheat seeds
    for row in range(9):
        if row // 3 != rest_strip:  # skip the resting strip
            plant_row(9)
            agent.move(BACK, 9)
        agent.move(RIGHT, 1)
    rest_strip = (rest_strip + 1) % 3
    player.say("Clucky: replant done! Strip " + str(rest_strip) + " is resting.")

player.on_chat("replant", on_chat)

📝 Pseudocode

BEGIN SugarMillLoop
  LOOP forever                         // repeating command block
    IF observer detects growth THEN
       piston breaks cane
    END IF
    WHILE hopper has items                 // comparator signal
       sugar ← sugar + 1
    END WHILE
    IF sugar ≥ 64 THEN
       stock ← stock + 1
       PLAY levelup ; SHOW "Shipment sent!"
       sugar ← 0
    ELSE
       SHOW "Sugar: " + sugar + " / 64"
    END IF
  END LOOP
END SugarMillLoop

BEGIN BuySugar(player)
  IF player.coins ≥ 6 AND stock ≥ 1 THEN
     GIVE sugar ×16 ; player.coins ← player.coins − 6 ; stock ← stock − 1
  ELSE
     SHOW "✖ Sold out / not enough coins"
  END IF
END BuySugar

Standard symbols: rounded = start/end, rectangle = process, diamond = decision, parallelogram = input/output. This matches the command blocks in the Sugar Mill tab exactly.

Fair Play & Caring for Country

Safe · ethical · inclusive

Automation isn't just about being fast. It's about who it's fair to: the players, the animals, the workers, and the land.

🐔

Humane animal systems

No mob-cramming and no grinders. Chicken Co is free-range: every chicken gets at least a 3 × 3 space, grass, light and water. Eggs are collected by a hopper under the nest straw, so no animals are harmed for the economy.

🧑‍🌾

Fair work for villagers

Every farmer villager has a bed, a window, a workstation and a schedule with time off. A team member worked out that the farm only runs reliably when worker conditions match real workplace standards. The game rewards being fair.

🌱

Rotational land care

The Rest Field is split into three strips. Each harvest, one strip rests. This idea comes from rotational land and fire management that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have practised on Country for tens of thousands of years. Our sign explains why resting land matters, without pretending to speak for any community.

🎰

Plinko without gambling

Plinko Palace looks like a casino but it isn't one. It runs on free Golden Feathers that you earn by playing, and these can't be bought with Cluckcoins. Prizes are cosmetic only. A sign at the bottom shows the bell curve the pegs make, so the game turns into a maths lesson.

🛡️

Safe, moderated zones

PvP only happens in the opt-in arena. Builds are protected by Education Edition Border and Deny blocks. mobgriefing and tntexplodes are off. Chaos Chicken Hour only picks players who opted in.

♿

Multi-sensory access

Every event gives 3 cues: text on screen, a sound, and a lamp. Zones use colour and shape. Paths are 3 blocks wide, with ramps instead of ladders. Signs are dark on light, with large lettering at eye level. The lift means stairs are never required.

🐞 Bug Board (our favourite disasters)

#1 The Wither Incident

Severity: CRITICAL · v0.2 · FIXED

Someone summoned withers and a whole wing of Chicken Co was lost. Fix: gamerule mobgriefing false, a Deny-block shell around the tower, and the summon command removed from player access.

#2 TNT Mountain

Severity: CRITICAL · v0.2 · FIXED

40,000 blocks of TNT. You can guess the rest. Fix: gamerule tntexplodes false and a restore from the v0.1 backup. This is why QA exports a version every Friday.

#3 Everyone, Every Second

Severity: HIGH · v0.2 · FIXED

Chaos Chicken Hour was edited to tp @a on a 0-tick delay, and the whole class was stuck in the centre for minutes. Fix: @r[tag=chaos] (one opted-in player) plus a 6000-tick delay.

#4 Charged for Nothing

Severity: MEDIUM · v0.3 · FIXED

The shop showed “not enough coins” after taking the coins. Fix: moved the unless branch first in the chain (see the Market tab).

#5 Redstone Spaghetti

Severity: LOW · v0.3 · FIXED

The golem's wiring showed through its chest. Fix: rerouted it up the back wall with a repeater. A designer's verdict in the log: keeping redstone hidden is “the most annoying thing ever”.

#6 Villager Strike

Severity: MEDIUM · v0.2 · FIXED

The potato farm villagers wouldn't work. Fix: beds, a workstation each and a path to daylight. (A sword was suggested and politely rejected.)
