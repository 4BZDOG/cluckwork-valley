## Blurbs
**One line:** An automated farm-and-factory valley in Minecraft Education where every machine is see-through and every chicken has a job title.

**50 words:** Cluckwork Valley is an automated factory and smart farm built in Minecraft Education by Cluckwork Studios. Watch observers harvest sugar cane, ride a 30-floor command-block elevator, and earn Cluckcoins at the Farm-to-Table Market before your shift ends. Every machine is see-through, so you can watch how automation works.

**100 words (for the school newsletter):** This term our Year 9 Computing Technology class formed a single 12-person game studio and built *Cluckwork Valley*, a fully automated farm-and-factory world in Minecraft Education. Observers and pistons harvest crops on their own. An inverted daylight sensor makes a giant iron golem's eyes glow at night. A 29-command-block chain runs a 30-storey elevator, and a scoreboard economy tracks every coin and crate. The team also designed for fairness: free-range chickens, villagers with real working conditions, opt-in PvP, and a rotational Rest Field inspired by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander land-care practices.
